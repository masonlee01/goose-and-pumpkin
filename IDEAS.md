# The Ideas List

Anything the boys think of goes here. Nothing is too silly — silly is the point.
Write it down even if it sounds impossible; a lot of impossible things turn out
to be about twenty minutes of work.

Add a line, any time. No particular format. Cross things off with `~~like this~~`
when they are in the game.
